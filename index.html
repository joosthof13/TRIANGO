    How to play

    Fill the entire board using three shapes.

    1. Every row, column and bold-bordered region must have equal counts of circles, squares and triangles.
    2. Never put three identical shapes together horizontally or vertically.
    3. Between cells, = means equal, × means different, and arrows follow the cycle circle → square → triangle → circle.
    4. In Master, both long diagonals also need equal numbers of each shape.
    5. Tap Pause to hide the entire board and freeze the timer until you resume. Paused games stay paused after reopening the app.
    6. Click an editable cell once for a circle, twice for a square, three times for a triangle, and a fourth time to clear it. Given shapes are fixed.
    7. Tap Hint to see a logically forced move and a step-by-step explanation. You can place it yourself or use Place this shape.

    Difficulty levels are calibrated from complete, proven deduction paths. Their logic-score bands do not overlap; individual solving time may still vary.

    Keyboard: 1 circle, 2 square, 3 triangle, ⌫ erase, arrow keys to move, Ctrl+Z undo.
