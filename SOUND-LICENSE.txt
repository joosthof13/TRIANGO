TRIANGO warm Cream-shape sound effects — third-party source attribution

The embedded circle, square and triangle audio is derived from sampled NovelKeys Cream switch
keypresses in kbsim by Thomas Lai:
https://github.com/tplai/kbsim
Original recordings: src/assets/audio/cream/press/GENERIC_R0.mp3 and GENERIC_R1.mp3

The source recordings were modified for TRIANGO (timing, EQ, transient shaping,
gentle layering, pitch and normalization). They are distributed under the MIT
license below. This license statement applies to these sound assets, not to
TRIANGO's other source code.
